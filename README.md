# Customer-Churn-Prediction

The primary objective of this project was to demonstrate a clear and practical approach to customer churn analysis and prediction — from understanding the underlying customer behaviour and defining churn, through to feature preparation, model development, evaluation, and interpretation.

Given the scope of the exercise, the focus was placed on the quality of the modelling logic and reasoning rather than extensive model optimisation. As a result, the analysis does not go deeply into areas such as exhaustive hyperparameter tuning, extensive model experimentation, or production-level model optimisation. These would be appropriate next steps in a longer-term modelling project, but were intentionally kept outside the scope here.

The intention was to demonstrate an end-to-end understanding of the churn problem and make sensible, explainable modelling decisions within the constraints of the exercise.
